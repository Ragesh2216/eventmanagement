Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ticketing, badges, and attendance.

How do I receive my event ticket & QR badge?
Upon completion of booking in your Client Portal, your encrypted digital pass with QR code will be available immediately and emailed to your registered address.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?
Full refunds are issued up to 14 days prior to event start. Within 14 days, pass transfers to another delegate or event credit are available.
Are dietary requirements accommodated for gala dinners?
Yes! You can specify vegan, kosher, halal, or allergy requirements directly within your Client Dashboard prior to the event.
